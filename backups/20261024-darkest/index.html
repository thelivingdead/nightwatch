          03 · About

          For centuries, London’s night watchmen walked the streets from dusk till dawn. Lantern in hand, they called the hours and kept watch over the sleeping city, standing between its people and the dangers of the dark.

          Night’s Watch carries that duty into the present. We study where trouble has been, using real police.uk crime records, and weigh it against distance and how walkable each street is, then light the safest path home. No lantern needed, just the quiet assurance that someone is keeping watch.
