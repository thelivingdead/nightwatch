                  About the data

                  Our data comes from police.uk. Locations are approximate, so each crime is shown near where it happened rather than at the exact spot. Records also arrive about a month late. Night Watch shows you what has happened, not what will happen tonight, so stay alert.

                    About

                    Every city needs someone watching over it after dark.

                    Night Watch began with a simple question: why should getting home at night feel like a gamble? So we built a map that uses real police records to see the city's dark corners, then plans a route through the streets that are busier, better lit and safer.

                    No capes, no masks. Just a watchful eye on the streets, so you can walk them with confidence.
